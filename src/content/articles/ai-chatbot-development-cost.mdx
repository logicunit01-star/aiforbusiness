### 2. Platform (Where It Will Run) 

-   Website chatbot → lower cost  
-   WhatsApp chatbot → medium cost  
-   Multi-platform (Web + App + Social) → high cost  

### 3\. Integrations 

Integrating with systems like: 

-   CRM  
-   Payment gateways  
-   Booking systems  

Adds 20%–50% more cost 

#### 4. AI Model Selection 

Using advanced AI like GPT-style models increases: 

-   Development cost  
-   API usage cost (monthly)

## FAQ 

**1\. How much does it cost to build an AI chatbot?** 

The cost typically ranges from $3,000 to $50,000+, depending on features, complexity, and integrations. Simple chatbots are cheaper, while advanced AI chatbots cost more. 

**2\. What factors affect chatbot development cost the most?** 

The biggest factors include:   
Features (AI, NLP, automation)    
Platform (website, WhatsApp, app)    
Integrations (CRM, APIs)    
Development team  

**3\. Can I build a chatbot on a low budget?** 

Yes. You can build a basic chatbot for under $5,000 by starting with limited features and scaling later. 

**4\. Is AI chatbot worth the investment?** 

Yes. AI chatbots reduce operational costs, improve response time, and increase conversions, making them a high-ROI investment. 

**5\. How long does it take to develop a chatbot?** 

Basic chatbot: 2–4 weeks    
Advanced AI chatbot: 2–6 months
